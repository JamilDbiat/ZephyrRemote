# Zephyr Remote

Control your Windows PC from your phone — from anywhere.

- Turn your PC on (Wake-on-LAN), off, sleep, restart, lock
- Open your PC apps and games from your phone, and close running ones
- Watch your screens live (60 fps), control the mouse with touch gestures, type with your keyboard
- Hear your PC's sound on your phone
- Send files both ways, and share the clipboard
- Works outside your home Wi-Fi, with a PIN, lockout after 3 wrong tries, and new-device approval
- Optional Discord bot alerts and links

## Install

1. Download **ZephyrRemoteSetup.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/JamilDbiat/ZephyrRemote/releases/latest).
2. Run it and click through. Zephyr starts by itself and opens a QR code.
3. Scan the QR code with your phone and create a PIN.

Zephyr starts with Windows and updates itself.

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Screen streaming is fastest with an NVIDIA, Intel or AMD GPU.
